## Ternary Bonsai 2 27B

PrismML's ternary Qwen3.8-27B: 1.72 bits per weight, 98.2% of FP16 quality on thinking-mode benchmarks, vision, tool use and a 262K context.

Needs the **Bonsai 2 · Prism llama.cpp** runtime. The PQ2_0 / PTQ1_0 packings are not supported by the stock llama.cpp runtimes.

Reasoning effort: **xhigh** (default) or **medium** for shorter answers. **low** is not trained and behaves close to xhigh.
